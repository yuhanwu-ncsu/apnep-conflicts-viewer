How to use

Conflict pairs show the spatial overlap between human-use pressures and ecological or community resources across the Albemarle-Pamlico region on an approximately 5 km² hex grid.

Choose a category and view above the map. Each hex receives a Low, Medium, or High class for pressure and resource. The nine colors combine those two classes; dark indigo indicates both are High.

Filter cells with the three switches at the bottom. Choose one at a time, or click the active switch to show all classified cells. Click a hex for its source values.

Explore the map with search, layers, basemap, zoom, and home controls. The search in this test uses a short list of named places; it does not provide address geocoding.

Data note: This test uses the local APNEP all_pairs export rendered as static Tippecanoe vector tiles. It is an independent preview and does not require ArcGIS Online at runtime.
